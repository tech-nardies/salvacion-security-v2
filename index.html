About the Agency

A Trusted Security Partner Since 2009

Salvation Security Agency Inc. is a Philippine security agency dedicated to protecting people, property, and daily operations. From commercial sites and residential communities to corporate gatherings, we deliver professional security services with discipline and care.

Our work is guided by the values marked on our seal: Integrity, Vigilance, and Excellence, and by a simple commitment: dependable protection, delivered professionally, every single day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

What security services does Salvation Security Agency Inc. offer?

We provide three core services: security guard deployment for commercial and corporate sites, residential and home security including patrols for gated communities and private estates, and event security management for corporate gatherings and large public functions. The agency also operates a dedicated Security Training Center.

Where is Salvation Security Agency Inc. located?

Our office is located at Villa Luisa Resort, Sitio Ambuklao, Pinasling, Gerona, Tarlac 2302, Philippines. Contact us to confirm coverage and deployment availability in your area.

How do I request a security services quotation?

You can request a quotation by calling (045) 608 0518, sending a message through our official Facebook page, or using the inquiry form below. Providing your site location, the type of service, the number of personnel needed, and your schedule helps us prepare an accurate quotation faster.

How are your security personnel selected and trained?

Our personnel are vetted for integrity, tactical awareness, and sound decision-making. They undergo rigorous training and operate under a strict chain of command, with supervisory oversight to keep every deployment reliable and accountable.

Do you provide security for private events?

Yes. Our event security team handles crowd management, VIP transport coordination, and emergency protocol execution for corporate galas, private functions, and large-scale public events.

How can I apply to join the Salvation Security team?

Visit our Careers page or send us a message through our official Facebook page to learn about current openings and application requirements.
